# WRTC 2030 Selection Rules v1.0 Officially Approved and Published

The WRTC 2030 Organizing Committee is pleased to announce that the **WRTC 2030 Selection Rules v1.0** have been officially approved by the WRTC Sanctioning Committee today, Monday, October 19, 2026, following the final verification process.

## Key Rules Highlights

- **24 Qualifying Events:** Spanning from December 2026 through November 2028.
- **First Qualifying Event:** The **9A DX Contest** (19–20 December 2026 UTC). Contesters can review contest rules and exchange details directly at the [9A DX Contest website](https://9adx-contest.com/).
- **Scores Calculation:** An operator's total qualification score is the sum of their **up to 12 best contest scores** across the qualification window.
- **Multi-Operator Cap:** At most 3 Multi-Operator contest scores may be counted toward the final qualification total.
- **Out-of-QA Cap:** At most 3 contests operated outside the applicant's home Qualifying Area may be counted.
