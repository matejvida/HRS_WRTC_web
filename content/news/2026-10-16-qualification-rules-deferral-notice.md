# Notice Regarding Final Verification of WRTC 2030 Selection Rules

The WRTC 2030 Organizing Committee wishes to inform the global radiosport community that the final verification and cross-regional harmonization of the **WRTC 2030 Selection Rules** is reaching its concluding stages in close coordination with the WRTC Sanctioning Committee.

## Ensuring Global Fairness

Over the past two weeks, detailed statistical reviews of contest participation patterns across all 6 IARU regions were conducted to verify:

1. **29 Qualifying Areas (QAs):** Ensuring proportional team representation for Europe (23 teams), North America (16 teams), Asia (5 teams), Africa (2 teams), South America (2 teams), and Oceania (2 teams).
2. **Special Teams Allocation:** Reserving 3 dedicated Youth Team slots, 4 Sponsored Team slots, 2 Wild Card slots, and 1 Defending Champion slot.
3. **Weighting Factor Balance:** Finalizing Table 2 single-operator and multi-operator multipliers for all 24 qualifying contests.

The official, fully verified rules document (Selection Rules v1.0) and Qualifying Events calendar will be released on **Monday, October 19, 2026**.
