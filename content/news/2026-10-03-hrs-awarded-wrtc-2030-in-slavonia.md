# HRS Awarded Organization of WRTC 2030 in Slavonia

The WRTC Sanctioning Committee (SC) announced during the Istra Contest Conference in Poreč, Croatia on Saturday, October 3, 2026, that the **Croatian Amateur Radio Association (Hrvatski radioamaterski savez — HRS)** has been selected to host the **World Radiosport Team Championship 2030**.

## Championship Overview

The tenth edition of the World Radiosport Team Championship will take place in **July 2030** in the northeastern region of **Slavonia**, centering on the host cities of **Osijek, Čepin, and Đakovo**.

- **60 Competing Teams:** 120 of the world's most accomplished contesters competing two-by-two.
- **Identical Operating Conditions:** Standardized antennas, transceivers, output power (100 W), and field stations pitched across the flat Pannonian plain.
- **Continuous 24-Hour Competition:** Coinciding with the prestigious IARU HF World Championship weekend.

The WRTC Organizing Committee expresses its profound gratitude to the international amateur radio community, the WRTC Sanctioning Committee, and the Croatian authorities for their trust and support.
