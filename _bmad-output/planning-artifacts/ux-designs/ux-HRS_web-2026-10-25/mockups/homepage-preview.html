OSIJEK · ČEPIN · ĐAKOVO | JULY 2030 | CALLSIGN PREFIX 9A

THE OLYMPICS OF AMATEUR RADIO ARRIVES IN CROATIA

The WRTC Sanctioning Committee has awarded the organization of WRTC 2030 to the Croatian Amateur Radio Association (HRS). In July 2030, 60 teams (120 of the world's top contesters) will gather in the northeastern region of Slavonia — Osijek, Čepin, and Đakovo.

All teams compete head-to-head continuously for 24 hours under identical technical conditions — creating a level playing field on the flat Pannonian plains where operating skill, strategy, teamwork, and endurance determine the world champions.

Host Country & Venue · Slavonia, Croatia

Osijek · Čepin · Đakovo

Level Playing Field

The Pannonian Plain Advantage

Situated in northeastern Croatia, the flat agricultural plains spanning Osijek, Čepin, and Đakovo allow all 60 field stations to be deployed with uniform elevation, unobstructed 360° horizons, and truly comparable HF propagation.

Culture & Heritage

Heart of Golden Slavonia

Visitors and competitors will experience Osijek's 18th-century baroque Tvrđa fortress on the Drava River, Đakovo's famous Cathedral and State Lipizzaner Stud Farm, Kopački Rit nature park, and renowned Slavonian hospitality.

9A Contesting Tradition

Organized by HRS (hamradio.hr)

Hosted by the Croatian Amateur Radio Association (HRS) and backed by Croatia's world-renowned 9A contesting community, kicking off qualification with the 9A DX Contest.
