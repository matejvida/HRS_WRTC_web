## Extracted Claims

1. **Origin & Purpose of `wrtc2030.hr`**

   - **Source:** `https://www.wrtc2030.hr` / `https://www.hamradio.hr`
   - **Publisher:** WRTC 2030 Organizing Committee / HRS
   - **Pub Date:** 2026-10-19
   - **Accessed:** 2026-10-25
   - **Confidence:** high
   - **Class:** features

2. **Structural Clone of `wrtc2026.org` Taxonomy**
   - **Claim:** The current `wrtc2030.hr` fast preview replicates the exact early-stage menu labels and page hierarchy of `wrtc2026.org`: `About Us` (`WRTC Team`, `News Items`, `Past Events`), `Competition` (`Selection Criteria`, `Selection Criteria Q&A`, `Qualification Rules Q&A`), and `Contact` (`wrtc2030@hamradio.hr`, `rules-wrtc2030@hamradio.hr`).
   - **Source:** `https://www.wrtc2030.hr`
   - **Publisher:** WRTC 2030 Organizing Committee
   - **Pub Date:** 2026-10
   - **Accessed:** 2026-10-25
   - **Confidence:** high (verified by side-by-side comparison with `wrtc2026.org` menu taxonomy)
   - **Class:** positioning

3. **Valuable Assets Currently on `wrtc2030.hr` (What to KEEP)**
   - **Claim:** `wrtc2030.hr` already contains authoritative, SC-approved core content that must be preserved verbatim or structurally migrated:
     1. Domain name (`wrtc2030.hr`) and official email routing (`wrtc2030@hamradio.hr`, `rules-wrtc2030@hamradio.hr`).
     2. Official **WRTC 2030 Selection Rules (v1.0, approved Oct 19, 2026)**, Weighting Factors (WF), Qualification Areas (QA) breakdown, and Score Calculation Examples.
     3. The **60-Team Allocation Policy** explanation (50 QA qualifiers + 1 Defending Champion + 4 Sponsored Teams + 3 Youth Teams + 2 Wild Cards) and its rationale.
     4. Official **Q&A Repository** (`Selection Criteria Q&A` and `Qualification Rules Q&A`), which shows transparency to the global contesting community.
     5. Complete **Organizing Committee Roster** with callsigns and roles (`9A4M`, `9A1UN`, `9A5W`, `9A3A`, `9A2G`, `9A5X`, `9A5R`, `9A6KX`, `9A3XV`, `YU1LA`, `9A6PAX`).
     6. Initial **News items** (Poreč announcement, Rules v1.0 release) and **Past Events** historical continuity.
   - **Source:** `https://www.wrtc2030.hr`
   - **Publisher:** WRTC 2030 Organizing Committee
   - **Pub Date:** 2026-10
   - **Accessed:** 2026-10-25
   - **Confidence:** high
   - **Class:** features

4. **Gaps & Weaknesses of `wrtc2030.hr` (What to DISCARD / REPLACE)**
   - **Claim:** As a fast preview, `wrtc2030.hr` has major functional, UX, and branding gaps that make it unsuitable as the permanent 2026–2030 platform without a ground-up redesign:
     1. **Lack of Croatian & Slavonian Identity:** It reads like a generic template clone of `wrtc2026.org` rather than a proud Croatian (`9A`) and Slavonian (Osijek/Čepin/Đakovo) showcase.
     2. **No Interactive Qualification Standings Engine:** With the first qualifying contest (`9A DX Contest`) happening December 19–20, 2026, static pages/PDFs cannot handle interactive leaderboard filtering by QA, sub-area, Youth, and callsign across 24 events.
     3. **Missing Sponsorship & Fundraising Funnel:** Increasing from 50 to 60 teams explicitly relies on 4 Sponsored Teams plus Tent/Station Sponsors, Commercial Sponsors, Club Sponsors, and Personal Donations — yet `wrtc2030.hr` currently lacks interactive sponsorship tiers, tent sponsorship reservation, or donation flows.
     4. **No Venue / Slavonia Experience Hub:** Competitors, referees, and families know Croatia for the Adriatic coast, not inland Slavonia; the preview lacks an interactive map of Osijek, Čepin, and Đakovo explaining *why* Slavonia's flat Pannonian plains offer world-class HF propagation and equality of operating sites, alongside cultural/gastronomic/travel guides.
     5. **Static Document-Dump UX:** Rules, score formulas, and Q&As are presented as dense text/PDF blocks rather than interactive tools (e.g., an interactive QA map, searchable FAQ, and qualifying contest calendar countdown).
   - **Source:** `https://www.wrtc2030.hr`
   - **Publisher:** Direct competitive gap analysis vs. `wrtc2026.org` and WRTC 2030 requirements
   - **Pub Date:** 2026-10-25
   - **Accessed:** 2026-10-25
   - **Confidence:** high
   - **Class:** features
