## Domain Glossary (Radiosport & Croatian Context)
- **WRTC:** World Radiosport Team Championship — quadrennial "Olympics of Amateur Radio Contesting" where all teams operate from identical field sites with identical antennas, tribanders/wires, and power limits (100W) using randomized callsigns and on-site referees.
- **HRS:** *Hrvatski radioamaterski savez* (Croatian Amateur Radio Association), the national IARU member society in Croatia.
- **9A:** Official ITU amateur radio callsign prefix for Croatia (central to Croatian ham identity, e.g., `9A DX Contest`).
- **QA (Qualification Area):** Global geographical divisions from which Team Leaders (TL) qualify based on normalized contest scores and Weighting Factors (WF).
- **TL / TM:** Team Leader (qualifies via QA rankings) and Team Mate (selected by the TL).
