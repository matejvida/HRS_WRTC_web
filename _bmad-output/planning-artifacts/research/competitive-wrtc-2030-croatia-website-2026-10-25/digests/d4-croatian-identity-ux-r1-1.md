2. **Synthesizing National Croatian Pride with Slavonian Regional Character**
   - **Claim:** Because WRTC 2030 is hosted in Slavonia (Osijek, Čepin, Đakovo) in July 2030, the visual and editorial identity must bridge two narratives:
     - **National Croatian Touch:** Modern, restrained geometric *šahovnica* (red/white checkerboard) micro-patterns blended with coaxial/RF wave or Morse (`···−` / `9A`) motifs; Croatian tricolor palette refined for modern web UI (Deep Adriatic Navy `#0B1F3A`, Croatian Crvena `#D62828`, Crisp White `#F8FAFC`).
     - **Slavonian Regional Touch:** Slavonia (`Zlatna Slavonija` — Golden Slavonia) brings Warm Slavonian Gold (`#D99B26` — golden wheat fields, oak forests, Đakovo Lipizzaners, Osijek Tvrđa baroque fortress, Drava river) and the **technical story of the Pannonian Basin**: flat, unobstructed terrain across Osijek, Čepin, and Đakovo that guarantees 60 truly identical HF propagation sites.
