1. EXECUTIVE SCOPE BOUNDARIES

What We Are Building Now vs. Later vs. Never

✓ Phase 1 MVP (Build Now — 2026)

  • Official Brand & Dual-Theme Shell: Official `wrtc2030.hr` SVG logos (Day & Night), Proposal 1 *"Kockice & 9A Airwaves"* hero, and neat red checker placements.
  • Interactive Qualification Standings (`/competition/standings/`): Filter by all 29 Qualifying Areas (QAs), Sub-Areas, callsign search, visual Cutoff Line, and Operator Score Breakdown Drawer.
  • Selection Rules v1.0 Hub (`/competition/selection-rules/`): All 3 tabs (Rules, Q&A, Score Examples), sticky TOC (Sections 1–11), Tables 1–7, and 3 official PDF downloads.
  • 24-Contest Qualifying Calendar (`/competition/qualifying-events/`): Full schedule from `#1 9A DX Contest` (Dec 19–20, 2026) to `#24 CQ WW CW` (Nov 2028).
  • Croatia & Slavonia Venue Guide (`/croatia-slavonia/`): Why Slavonia's flat Pannonian plain is ideal for 60 identical stations + Osijek, Đakovo, Čepin & Kopački Rit guide.
  • News, OC Roster (11 Members), Past WRTCs (1990–2026) & Clean Static Sponsors Page.

⏳ Phase 2 (Deferred to 2028–2030)

  • Team Leader Application Portal (Late 2028): Needed after Qualifying Event #24 (Nov 2028) when qualified Team Leaders formally apply and name their teammates.
  • Sponsored Team Applications (Jan 2029): Per Rule 8.D, the application process for the 4 Sponsored Teams opens after 1 January 2029.
  • Referee, Volunteer & Pileup Portals (2029–2030): Operational forms and pileup award leaderboards for Championship Week.
  • Live July 2030 Scoreboard: Real-time 24-hour contest scoreboard during the championship weekend in Slavonia.

✕ Explicitly Excluded (Not Needed)

  • No Interactive 60-Tent Picking Grid: Visitors and sponsors do not click or reserve individual tents on a map.
  • No Crowdfunding Bars or Online Bidding Widgets: No fundraising progress bars, live auctions, or payment checkouts on the site.
  • No External Contest Log Adjudication: Raw contest logs (`Cabrillo`) are adjudicated by each contest sponsor; the website imports the official calculated standings snapshots.
  • No Public User Login Required: All standings, breakdowns, and rules are openly accessible without accounts.
2. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (12 CORE CAPABILITIES)

Detailed Feature Requirements (FR-1 through FR-12)

FR-1 Global Header & Footer

Official WRTC 2030 Croatia SVG Logo & Day | Night Theme

Uses the exact official `wrtc-logo.svg` (Day) and `wrtc-logo-white.svg` (Night) from `wrtc2030.hr` with a zero-flash Day/Night theme switcher.

  • Preserves `#273B8F` Royal Blue, `#ED1C24` Checker Red, and `#0D1333` Shack Navy.
  • Remembers user preference in `localStorage` (`wrtc2030-theme`).
FR-2 Design System (Proposal 1)

Croatian Checker Motifs ("Dynamic Kockice & 9A Airwaves")

Weaves the Croatian red checker pattern neatly across the site without visual clutter.

  • Hero features stepped red/white Kockice mosaic + concentric `9A` radio propagation rings.
  • Includes `2×2` `.checker` kickers, `5-square` `.checker-five` card marks (matching the logo), and `12px` `.checker-ribbon` section dividers.
FR-3 /competition/standings/

29 Qualifying Areas (QA) Filter, Callsign Search & Cutoff Line

Interactive leaderboard allowing contesters to track qualification across all 29 global regions (`EU #1–#9`, `NA #1–#11`, `AS #1–#4`, `AF`, `SA #1–#2`, `OC #1–#2`) and Youth.

  • Instant (`< 50ms`) filter by QA, Sub-Area, or operator callsign (`9A5W`, `DL1AAA`).
  • Automatically draws the red **Qualification Cutoff Line** below Rank $N$ for the selected QA's team quota.
  • Deep-linkable URLs (`?qa=EU-6&call=9A`) for sharing on reflectors.
FR-4 /competition/standings/

Operator Score Breakdown Drawer & Pre-Season View

Clicking any operator row opens a detailed breakdown of the contests counted toward their Qualification Score.

  • Shows each counted contest, mode (`CW`/`SSB`/`Mixed`), category (`Single-Op`/`Multi-Op`), weighting factor, and score points.
  • Prior to Event #1 adjudication, displays a clean **Pre-Season Quota & Countdown View**.
FR-5 /competition/selection-rules/

Selection Rules v1.0, Q&A, Score Examples & 3 PDFs

Preserves 100% of the official Selection Rules v1.0 document, Q&A, and Score Calculation Examples with a sticky sidebar.

  • 3 switchable tabs + collapsible numbered Table of Contents (`Sections 1–11`).
  • All 7 official tables rendered with monospace callsign/numeric alignment.
  • Direct download card for the 3 official PDF files.
FR-6 /competition/qualifying-events/

Interactive 24-Contest Qualifying Calendar (2026–2028)

Visual schedule of all 24 Qualifying Events from `#1 9A DX Contest` (Dec 19–20, 2026) to `#24 CQ WW DX CW` (Nov 2028).

  • Filter by status (`Upcoming` vs `Completed / Standings Published`), mode, and Table 2 weight.
  • Powers the live **"Next Qualifying Event"** banner on the homepage.
FR-7 /croatia-slavonia/

Slavonia Propagation Arena & 60-Station Technical Profile

Showcases why northeastern Croatia (Osijek, Čepin, Đakovo) was chosen as the championship arena.

  • Highlights the flat Pannonian plain (0° take-off angle, 85–110m ASL elevation) and 100% identical 60-station field setup.
FR-8 /croatia-slavonia/

Host Cities (`Osijek · Čepin · Đakovo`) & Visitor Guide

Welcomes international competitors, referees, and visiting families to Slavonia and Croatia.

  • Features Osijek (Tvrđa baroque fortress, Drava riverfront), Đakovo (Cathedral & Lipizzaner Stud Farm), Čepin, and Kopački Rit Nature Park, plus airport/highway arrival routes.
FR-9 /news/ & /feed.xml

Structured News & Official Announcements Feed

Chronological news hub with individual article permalinks, category tags, social preview cards, and RSS feed.

  • Seeded with the Oct 3, 2026 Poreč announcement, Oct 16 notice, and Oct 19 Selection Rules v1.0 approval.
FR-10 /sponsors/

Clean, Dignified Sponsors & Partners Recognition

Static tier recognition modelled on `wrtc2026.org` without any crowdfunding or tent-picking gimmicks.

  • Recognizes Institutional Host (`HRS`), Commercial Partners, Club & DX Foundations, and Individual Donors, with inquiry CTA to `wrtc2030@hamradio.hr`.
FR-11 / & /about/

Organizing Committee Roster (11) & Past WRTC Archive (1990–2026)

Preserves the complete 11-member WRTC 2030 Organizing Committee (`9A4M` Mate Botica and team) and historical archive of all 9 past WRTC editions.

FR-12 scripts/import-standings.ts

One-Step Validated Standings Upload (CSV / JSON)

Allows the WRTC OC IT/Scoring team (`9A3XV` / `9A5W`) to publish updated standings after each contest by dropping a CSV or JSON file.

  • Automated `Zod` schema validation verifies all 29 QAs, quotas, and scores at build time so broken data can never go live.
FR-13 Global Widget + /api/chat

AI Assistant (WRTC Rules & Croatia/Slavonia Concierge)

On-page AI Assistant that answers questions about website content/rules as well as Croatia & Slavonia tourism, accommodations, and local food.

  • Answers Selection Rules v1.0, Q&A, and 24-contest questions (citing exact rule sections).
  • Answers Osijek, Đakovo, Čepin & Croatia tourism, hotel/accommodation, food (*kulen*, *fiš paprikaš*, *čobanac*), and airport travel questions.
FR-14 netlify.toml + GitHub Actions

GitHub → Netlify CI/CD Deployment Pipeline

Automated build, data schema verification, testing, and deployment to Netlify directly from GitHub.

  • Runs TypeScript typecheck, `Zod` standings validation, and tests on every push/PR before publishing Preview & Production builds to Netlify.
3. QUALITY & TECHNICAL STANDARDS (NFR-1 TO NFR-3)

Performance, Accessibility & Architecture

NFR-1: Speed & Traffic Spikes

Built with Next.js 15 Static Generation (SSG). Every page is pre-rendered to static edge HTML (`< 200ms` load time worldwide) so post-contest traffic spikes cost nothing and never crash a database.

NFR-2: Callsign Legibility & WCAG AA

All callsigns (`9A4M`, `DJ5MW`), QA codes, and scores use `JetBrains Mono` with slashed zero (`0` vs `O`) and `tabular-nums` alignment. Both Day and Night modes pass WCAG 2.1 AA contrast.

NFR-3: Zero Tracking Cookies

Operates with zero third-party tracking cookies, meaning visitors are never interrupted by annoying GDPR cookie popups when checking standings or rules.
